3. Visualizing Backpropagation

Backpropagation uses the Derivative (slope) to decide how much to change the weights. Click the graph to "Drop" a weight, then watch it learn!

Understanding the Visuals:

  • Tangent Line (Black): This is the gradient. It shows the slope at your current weight.
  • Path (Pink/Blue): The colorful trail shows exactly how your weight "jumped" during optimization.
  • High Learning Rate: If $\eta$ is too high, the ball will bounce over the minimum (Overshooting)!
